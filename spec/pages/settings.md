# Settings

Settings holds the appearance, editor, keyboard shortcuts, language servers, files, agents and voice, tools and the project's own settings.

## Shows
- Appearance (theme, font size, Geist fonts), Editor, Keyboard (every shortcut, recordable), Languages (each language server: off, command, options), Files, Agents (who leads, Claude's model and effort, the team (each role's model or Off, and Document decisions as we work), Codex, automatic task effort and local routing, the agents' own browser, voice, cloud agents and their keys, Hands, iPhone and iPad: the companion server, and the morning update), Tools (versions and install commands) and Project.
- Agents says when Claude Code isn't installed, under Claude's model.
- The agents' own browser (on by default): the agent in charge and every job get browser_ tools (open, read, click, type, back, resize, screenshot) backed by a private WebKit browser inside `dante mcp`. It visits only the app on this Mac (localhost, 127.0.0.1, ::1, *.localhost, *.test) and hosts listed under `browser: hosts:` in .dante/project.yaml (and their subdomains); a link or form that leaves them is stopped and the agent told, and a page can't send anything to other hosts (fetch, XHR, WebSockets, beacons, frames and pop-ups are blocked; pictures, styles, scripts and fonts from a CDN still load). It keeps no cookies or sign-ins between runs and never uses the person's browser. Each read gives the page's text, numbered controls, page errors (console.error, uncaught errors, resources that didn't load) and failed fetch/XHR calls. Screenshots are kept a week in Application Support/Dante/browser/<project>. Its steps never ask, at any approval level or in away mode, since nothing leaves the Mac. Off for remote projects.
- Hands (off by default): lets the agent in charge open apps, find folders, read the window in front (its text and numbered controls, through Accessibility; password fields never read), press a control or choose a menu item by name, use the keyboard and mouse, and in Microsoft Outlook read recent mail and a day's calendar and write a draft email or reply that opens for the person to send (Hands never send); when on, the kill switch (⌃⌥⌘. anywhere, or Stop on the badge) and whether macOS allows Dante to control the Mac, with Open Accessibility Settings… when it doesn't.
- iPhone and iPad (off by default): when on, where Dante listens (this Mac's Tailscale address and port 47823), Show Pairing Code… (a QR code in a sheet, with Copy Link for pasting into the app, and Unpair All Devices…) and each connected device; without a Tailscale address it says to install Tailscale instead. Devices also get the Mac's recent projects: with nothing open, the phone lists them to open, and a spoken or typed question goes to the most recent one, which the Mac opens in a window first (up to 15 s) before asking its lead. Devices can also explore a project (open or recent, without opening a window): its docs, pictures, diagrams, system map and infrastructure, drawn here at the device's size and sent a page at a time, never code or env files; an unchanged page is answered by hash.
- Morning update: Greet me as (a first name), Include important mail from Outlook and Include today's meetings from Outlook (both off by default; they read Outlook on this Mac through AppleScript, which macOS asks about once; Outlook must be open and the new Outlook doesn't answer scripts), Always tell me about mail from (names or addresses, shown when mail is on) and Try it › Show the Update, which gathers it now and shows it in a sheet. The update is asked for by voice on the Mac or a device ("give me my morning update", "what did I miss", "brief me", "good morning"), or from Today on the iPhone and iPad. It covers up to eight open and recent git projects since 06:00 the day before (Friday on a Monday): the three busiest as sentences (who made how many changes and the newest commit subjects, your own commits as "you", by every git name and address configured), the rest counted; failing tests and critical security findings in any project; GitHub Actions runs (through gh) and the Azure DevOps runs Dante already keeps, where a failure a later pass on the same pipeline and branch cleared isn't mentioned; up to five important emails (scored without a model: flagged, high priority, people over notifications and newsletters, questions and deadlines; only sender, subject and first sentence leave Outlook); and today's meetings (how many, the first or the one on now, overlaps, all-day items). It's made at most every five minutes unless refreshed, and devices keep the last one. On an iPad, Wall shows it full screen with a clock and the agents' state, refreshed every 15 minutes, and can listen for "Hey Dante" on the iPad.
- Replies by voice: with mail on, saying "reply to Ana that Thursday works" (also "respond to", "answer Ana's email and say", "write back to") on the Mac, iPhone or iPad finds the newest email from that person in the last three days (by their name or address, newsletters and notifications left out) and opens a reply in Outlook, "Hi Ana," then what was said as a sentence and the Greet me as name, above the quoted message. Dante says who it's for and what about; it never sends. With mail off it says to turn it on here; with no match it says it couldn't find a recent email from them.

## Actions
- Change any setting; record a shortcut; reset to defaults.
- “Hey Dante” (off by default): while it's on the microphone stays open and speech is recognised on this Mac. Turn it on or off in Settings › Agents (Listen for “Hey Dante”), with the menu bar panel's Hey Dante button (filled while listening), the menu bar item's right-click menu or View › Listen for “Hey Dante”; turning it off closes the microphone at once unless you're talking.
- Hold ⌃⌥Space in any app to talk; release to send. Repeated key-down events don't restart recording. A quick release during permission/startup cancels the hold without opening the microphone later. Click Talk for the separate pause-to-send conversation mode.
- Paste a cloud agent's key (kept in the Keychain).
- Type a tool's install command into the terminal (never run by Dante).
- Project: move .dante to the Trash, set up again, forget saved connections.

## Edge cases
- A shortcut reset to its default can need a restart (noted beside it).
- Claude's effort (Claude Code's default, low, medium, high) is passed as `--effort`; changing it, the model or a team role's model starts Claude Code again on the next message, continuing the same conversation. The chat footer's model chip ("Opus 5.5  Medium", a bolt when fast mode is on) opens the model picker, laid out like Claude Code's own: "Model · from the next message", then Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Fable 5.1 (with a "Requires usage credits" pill; passed as its full id) and Haiku 5.5, the chosen one ticked and the others showing the number key (1–4) that picks them while the picker is open; Effort with the current level and a flyout to the left (Default, Low, Medium, High); More models (Claude Code's default); and Fast mode with an Enable fast mode switch, passed to Claude Code as `fastMode` in `--settings`, Opus only and billed as extra usage, so it's greyed out on other models. The chip names the model Claude Code reports running when it has started ("Opus 4.6" on an older Claude Code), else the chosen one. When Codex leads, the same picker lists Codex's first four models from its model/list, the rest and Codex's default under More models, and Effort holds Automatic by task, Default and the model's reasoning levels; it has no fast mode. Choices are saved for every window and apply from the next message.
- The chat footer shows what the last reply cost ("last 1.8M"): each step of a reply re-reads the whole conversation, so steps × context; its tooltip and the usage popover explain it and suggest compacting when the conversation is over 100k tokens.
- Automatic task effort (on by default) sorts each request on the Mac without spending tokens: anything mentioning security, auth, migrations, production, deploys, deletion and the like is complex; a few routine openings ("rename …", "fix the typo") are routine; the rest is standard. Codex runs routine work at low reasoning and the rest at medium; Claude keeps its chosen model. A self-contained rewrite or summary ("Summarise this: …", no attachments, nothing about the project or the conversation) goes to the smallest suitable Ollama model first, and to the agent after 30 seconds or on any failure. Each decision is a note in the conversation. Choosing a reasoning level in the model picker's Effort while Codex leads turns it off; "Automatic by task" turns it back on.
- Click-to-talk and wake-word voice send after one second without a transcript change. Holding the talk shortcut disables that silence timer; recognition segments are joined until release. Release stops capture immediately and waits for the recognizer's final text, up to 600 ms, before sending once. Held turns don't automatically listen for a follow-up. A completed reply sentence is released for synthesis after 200 ms of stable text, without waiting for another sentence or for the turn to end; incomplete text and abbreviations wait.
- Natural speech uses chunks of at most 180 characters, split between words (a single longer word stays whole). Playback overlaps synthesis, with at most three outstanding chunks: one playing and two being prepared or ready. Cancelling speech clears queued audio and prevents requests cancelled during warm-up from being submitted.
- Spoken requests ask the current agent for brief, conversational replies, with long details on screen. The chosen model, reasoning level and approval rules still apply.
- When continued conversation is on, follow-up listening resumes once both the agent turn and its audio are finished, even if a short streamed answer finished playing before the turn ended.

## Acceptance
- Settings apply without a restart unless they say otherwise.
- The companion server listens only on the Tailscale address and refuses any device that can't prove it holds the pairing key; unpairing makes a new key.
- Hands' keyboard, mouse, screenshot, window-reading, menu and Outlook steps ask every time at any approval level, jobs never get Hands, and Hands never type or click in Dante, terminals, System Settings or password apps.
- While the agent uses Hands a badge floats at the top of the screen saying what it did, with Stop; Stop or ⌃⌥⌘. refuses every further step at once (typing stops mid-word), turns Hands off and stops the conversation.
